Add your courses

  1. In EduGate open السجل الأكاديمي (Academic Record).
  2. Print it and choose Save as PDF. Clear photos or screenshots also work, one per page.
  3. Upload it, check every row, then import.

Your file is sent securely to Cloudflare Workers AI only to read it. This site doesn't keep the file, and nothing is added until you import.
