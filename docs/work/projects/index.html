Software Project Management — Fursah

Software Engineering

A software project management study for Fursah, accompanied by a linked prototype. The course project develops an incremental, artifact-based plan that is refined as scope, risks, and stakeholder needs evolve.

Focus
Project PlanningRisk ManagementWBSSchedulingStakeholder Analysis
Project WebsitePrototypeRequirements
Objectives & deliverables

The brief covers project scope and organization, comparison of two plan-driven and two Agile methodologies, risk analysis, work breakdown and scheduling, stakeholder communications, and progress metrics.

Project objectives
  • Define the problem or opportunity, intended benefits, stakeholders, team structure, and a detailed, non-redundant project scope.
  • Compare two plan-driven and two Agile methodologies against the project context; justify the selection and assess methodology-specific and wider project risks.
  • Estimate the work using a consistent WBS and develop either a justified Gantt schedule or an estimated product backlog and a detailed Sprint 1 backlog, depending on the selected methodology.
  • Analyze stakeholder power and interest, plan who communicates what and when, and define at least four metrics with a collection method and rationale.
  • Refine the four artifacts together using instructor and peer feedback, maintaining a cohesive plan for a ten-minute final presentation.
Required deliverables
  • Project overview, organizational structure, and scope
  • Methodology comparison, selection, and risk register
  • Work breakdown structure, estimates, and schedule
  • Stakeholder register, communications plan, and metrics
  • Integrated artifacts and final presentation
