Packet Filtering Firewall

Notes

  1. 1

Slide 27 / 41

Circuit-Level Gateway

Notes

  1. 1

    Circuit-level gateway (session layer): validates the TCP handshake, not the content.

Slide 28 / 41

Application Level Gateways

Notes

  1. 1

    Application-level gateway (proxy, layer 7): inspects content for specific applications.

Slide 29 / 41

Stateful Multilayer Inspection Firewall

Notes
