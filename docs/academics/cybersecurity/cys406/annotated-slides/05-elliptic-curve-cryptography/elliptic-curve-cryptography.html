Elliptic Curve Cryptography

Elliptic curves and their properties, trapdoor functions, groups and point addition, finite fields, scalar multiplication and cyclic subgroups, the elliptic-curve discrete logarithm problem, ECC encryption, ECDH key exchange, and why ECC gives the same security with smaller keys.

WHY USE ECC?

Notes

  1. 1

    A 256-bit ECC key ≈ 3072-bit RSA security: smaller keys, faster, less power and bandwidth (good for mobile and IoT).
