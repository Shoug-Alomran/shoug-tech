Honeypots and Proxy Servers

Honeypots and their types (low, medium and high interaction, production vs. research), honeypot tools, then proxy servers: benefits, how they work, proxy vs. packet filter, transparent, non-transparent, SOCKS, anonymous and reverse proxies, configuration and limitations.

    1. Honeypot: a decoy system with no production value; any interaction with it is suspicious. Used to detect and study attackers.
    🎯 How this can come up · 1 question
    MCQ

    The purpose of a honeypot is to:

    Correct: Lure attackers so they can be detected and studied.

    Exam weight ●●● High
    1. Low-interaction: emulates a few services (safe, limited data). High-interaction: real OS and services (rich data, more risk). Production vs. research honeypots.
    🎯 How this can come up · 1 question
    MCQ

    Which honeypot gives the most information but carries the most risk?

    Correct: High-interaction.
