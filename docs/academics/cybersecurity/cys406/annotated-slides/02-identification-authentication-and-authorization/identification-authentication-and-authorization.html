Identification, Authentication and Authorization

Access-control terminology and principles, the DAC, MAC, RBAC and rule-based models, identity and access management, the types of authentication (password, two-factor, biometrics, smart cards, SSO), authorization and accounting.

        1. Identification (claim who you are) → Authentication (prove it) → Authorization (what you may do) → Accounting (log what you did).

        Exam weight ●●● High
        1. Principles: least privilege, separation of duties, need to know.
        🎯 How this can come up · 1 question
        MCQ

        Giving users only the access they need for their job is:

        Correct: Least privilege.

        Exam weight ●●● High
        1. DAC: the owner decides access. MAC: the system decides by labels/clearances (military). RBAC: by job role. Rule-based: by rules (e.g. time of day).
        🎯 How this can come up · 2 questions
        MCQ

        Access decided by security labels and clearance levels is:

        Correct: MAC.

        MCQ

        A file owner granting a colleague read access is:

        Correct: DAC.

        Examples of MAC include Security-Enhanced Linux (SELinux) and Trusted Solaris
