Cryptography Basics: DES and AES

Cryptography and encryption, symmetric vs. asymmetric encryption, government access to keys, ciphers (block and stream), DES and its Feistel structure, and AES (Rijndael): key expansion and the four round transformations.

Symmetric Encryption

Notes

  1. 1

    Symmetric: ONE shared secret key for encryption and decryption. Fast; the problem is key distribution.

Exam angle

How many keys do n users need for pairwise symmetric encryption?

  1. n(n − 1)/2
  2. n
  3. 2n
  4. n²
Show answer

A. n(n − 1)/2

Asymmetric Encryption

Notes

Notes

Advanced Encryption Standard (AES)

Notes

  1. 1

AES Structure- Round

Notes

  1. 1

    Each round: SubBytes (S-box substitution), ShiftRows (permutation), MixColumns (mixing), AddRoundKey (XOR with the round key). The last round skips MixColumns.

  2. 2

    Memory trick Sub, Shift, Mix, Add.

  2. ShiftRows

  4. AddRoundKey
