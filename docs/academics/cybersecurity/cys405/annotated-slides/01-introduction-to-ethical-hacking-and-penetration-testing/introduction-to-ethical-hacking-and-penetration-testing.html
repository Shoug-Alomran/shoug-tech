Notes

Comparison among Audit, Vulnerability Assessment and Pen Testing

Notes

  1. 1

    Audit: checks compliance with a standard. Vulnerability assessment: finds and lists weaknesses. Pen test: actually exploits them to prove impact.

  2. 2

    Trap A vulnerability assessment does NOT exploit; a penetration test does.

Exam angle

PredictionQ1
