These are the pairs and trios that get mixed up. The question under each title is the one that tells them apart.

Hash vs MAC vs digital signature

Ask yourselfIs there a key, and could the sender deny it?

ConceptTell-tale signExample
HashNo key: integrity onlySHA-256
MACShared secret key: integrity + authenticationHMAC
Digital signaturePrivate key signs: adds non-repudiationRSA signature
StudyBreakdownCh 6 part 1SlidesCh 6 part 1AnnotatedCh 6 part 1SlidesCh 6 part 2AnnotatedCh 6 part 2
Quick testWhich mechanism lets a receiver prove to a judge who sent the message?

Digital signature (non-repudiation).

Encrypt vs sign: which key?

Ask yourselfDo you want secrecy or proof of the sender?

ConceptTell-tale signExample
EncryptReceiver's public key; receiver decrypts with their private keyOnly Bob can read it
SignSender's private key; anyone verifies with the sender's public keyProves Alice wrote it
StudyBreakdownCh 6 part 1SlidesCh 6 part 1AnnotatedCh 6 part 1

The sender's private key.
