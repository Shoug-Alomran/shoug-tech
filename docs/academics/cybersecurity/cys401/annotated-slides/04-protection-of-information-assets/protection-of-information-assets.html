Slide 16 / 71

ASP: An Application Service Provider (ASP) is a company that delivers software applications over the internet. Instead of hosting the software on your own servers, the ASP hosts and maintains it on their servers.

Notes

  1. 1

    ASP (Application Service Provider): delivers applications over the internet from its own servers. The organization stays accountable for its data → review the provider’s security and set an SLA with security requirements, responsibilities and breach notification.

Exam angle

Past exam · Major C

B · what is an ASP and what to do to protect data

PredictionQ1

What is an ASP, and what should be done to protect data when using one? (Major C)

Show answer

An Application Service Provider hosts and maintains software on its own servers and delivers it to customers over the internet. The organization remains accountable for its data, so it must review the provider’s security (due diligence) before engaging, and sign an SLA covering security requirements, responsibilities and breach notification, then audit the provider’s controls.

Slide 17 / 71

Due Care vs. Due Diligence

Notes

  1. 1

    Due care: the reasonable steps taken to protect assets (enforce passwords, patch, train). Due diligence: the research and assessment that finds what is needed (risk assessments, audits).

  2. 2

    Tip Due care = DOING the right thing (the protective action: training, patching, responding to a breach). Due diligence = RESEARCHING and checking (risk assessments, investigating threats, auditing vendors before access).

Slide 38 / 71

Common terms associated with Digital Data Disposal:

Notes

  1. 1

    Erasing = delete (recoverable). Formatting = new file structure (mostly recoverable). Clearing/overwriting = beats normal recovery tools (media can be reused). Purging = stronger (degaussing, crypto-erase). Destruction = most secure.

  2. 2

    Tip Weakest → strongest: erasing (delete, recoverable) · formatting · clearing/overwriting (beats normal recovery tools) · purging (degaussing, crypto-erase) · destruction (shred, incinerate, disintegrate, pulverize).
