PredictionQ2

A system is offline 2 h 20 min a week for 13 weeks for testing. What is its availability over those 13 weeks (1 d.p.)? (Quiz 1 222 style)

Compare with the model answer

Downtime = 140 min × 13 = 1820 min. Total time = 13 weeks × 7 × 24 × 60 = 131,040 min. Availability = (131,040 − 1,820) / 131,040 = 98.6%.
