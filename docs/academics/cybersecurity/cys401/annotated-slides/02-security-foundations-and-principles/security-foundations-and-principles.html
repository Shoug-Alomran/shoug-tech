Security Foundations and Principles

The three pillars, vulnerability vs. threat vs. risk, malware and the threat landscape, social engineering (phishing, spear phishing, tailgating), the C.I.A. triad with the attacks on each and the methods that protect it, the AAA services, and the protection mechanisms. Final 221 A/B, Quiz 1 222 and Majors A–C all draw on this deck.
