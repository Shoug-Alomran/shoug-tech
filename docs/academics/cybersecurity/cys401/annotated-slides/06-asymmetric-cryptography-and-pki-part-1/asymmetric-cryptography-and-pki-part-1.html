RSA: Key Generation

Notes

  1. 1

    Tip RSA in 5 lines: pick primes p, q · n = p·q · φ(n) = (p−1)(q−1) · pick e coprime with φ(n) · d = e⁻¹ mod φ(n). Encrypt C = Mᵉ mod n; decrypt M = Cᵈ mod n.

RSA with p = 3, q = 11, e = 7. Find n, φ(n), d, and encrypt M = 2.

Slide 28 / 37

Hash Functions

Notes

  1. 1

    Uses: storing passwords, integrity checks, signatures.

Exam angle

Past exam · Final 221 · B

written · two differences between hashing and encryption

PredictionQ1

How is hashing different from encryption? Give two differences. (Final 221 B)

Compare with the model answer

Hashing is one-way (no key, cannot be reversed) while encryption is reversible with a key; a hash always has a fixed-length output, while ciphertext length depends on the input.
