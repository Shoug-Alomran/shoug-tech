Quantitative Risk Assessments

Notes

  1. 1

    SLE = AV × EF · ARO = how many times a year · ALE = SLE × ARO · Value of a control = ALE(before) − ALE(after) − annual cost of the control.

  2. 2

    Tip SLE = AV × EF · ARO = how many times a year · ALE = SLE × ARO · Value of a control = ALE(before) − ALE(after) − annual cost of the control.

  3. 3

    Seen again Chapter 2: Risk Management Fundamentals, slide 30: the same formulas

Exam angle

PredictionQ1

ALE is $64,000 a year. A mitigation service cuts attacks to 80% of the original number and costs $1,000/month + $1,500 installation. What is the control’s value in year one?

Show answer

ALE after = 64,000 × 0.8 = $51,200. Annual cost = 12 × 1,000 + 1,500 = $13,500. Value = 64,000 − 51,200 − 13,500 = −$700, so in the first year the control costs more than it saves.
