ROUTE_PLAN // Contemporary Financial Transactions

Final roadmap

Every ruling in ISC213 comes back to the same few reasons: riba, gharar, gambling and injustice. The route starts with the principles that create those reasons, then applies them to rights and insurance, to investing, to financing a purchase, and finally to gains that are forbidden outright.
