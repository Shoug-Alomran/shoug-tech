L10 · Blocks 2–4

1. Compare covered, instant-credit and revolving cards in how they are funded, repaid and renewed, and give the ruling on each.

Show model answer

Covered (prepaid): funds are loaded before use and purchases come off the balance; the limit renews only when more funds are loaded. Permissible, because it works like cash and involves no loan (qard).

Instant credit (charge card): the issuer pays first and the balance is due in full by a deadline, such as 40 days; the limit returns only when it is settled. Permissible if free of riba conditions and late penalties.

Revolving credit: repaid in installments subject to interest; the limit renews with ongoing payments. Not permitted, because it involves riba on deferred payments.

L10 · Blocks 5–6

2. Name the five parties to a card transaction and describe the steps from purchase to collection.

Show model answer

Parties (MAGIC): Merchant, Acquirer (the merchant’s bank), Global organization (e.g. Visa or Mastercard), Issuer (the client’s bank) and Cardholder.

Steps: the cardholder presents the card; the merchant records the details and gives a receipt; the merchant submits the month’s receipts to its bank; that bank requests clearance through the global organization with the issuing bank; the issuer transfers the funds, typically in US dollars, to the global organization at the applicable exchange rate; the global organization pays the merchant’s bank; the issuer collects from the cardholder.

L10 · Blocks 7–8

3. State the conditions that make a credit card permissible, and explain why intending to pay within the grace period does not rescue a card that stipulates interest.

Show model answer

A card is permissible if free of three elements (PAR): a Penalty or extra charge for late payment, an Above-actual-cost charge for cash withdrawals, and Revolving credit.

Fiqh Council resolution 108 (1/12) says it is not permissible to issue or use a non-covered card if riba or interest is stipulated, even if the holder is determined to pay within the free grace period. The ruling looks at the condition in the contract, not at the holder’s intention.

L10 · Blocks 8–9

4. Explain what the Fiqh Council permits and forbids regarding issue fees, merchant commission, cash withdrawals, and buying gold, silver or currencies.

Show model answer

Issue and renewal fees: permitted as set fees on the basis of the real cost of the services.

Merchant commission: permitted so long as the business sells at the same price as for cash.

Cash withdrawals: a loan from the issuer. Acceptable if it leads to no interest and set fees have nothing to do with the amount or period. Anything above the actual cost is haram because it is riba.

Gold, silver and currencies: not permissible to buy with the uncovered card, because of the delayed payment.

L10 · Block 10

5. Is it permissible to work in marketing credit cards? Support your answer with the lecture’s evidence.

Show model answer

It depends on the card. Marketing a permissible type, free of haram matters, is fine. Marketing a haram type is not permissible, because that helps in sin and transgression.

The evidence is the hadith narrated by Muslim (1598) from Jaabir: the Messenger of Allah ﷺ cursed the one who consumes riba and the one who pays it, the one who writes it down and the two who witness it, and said they are all the same. Everyone who enables riba shares the sin.
