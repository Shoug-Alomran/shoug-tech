01 What gambling is (الميسر)

The wagering of money or something of material value on an event with an uncertain outcome, with the primary intent of winning additional money or material goods.
Betting Gambling
Definition Risking a sum of money on the outcome of a future event Playing games of chance for money
Whose effort decides it Other people’s effort The game of chance itself
SLIDE BLOCK 2

02 The forms it takes (أنواع الميسر)

Casino games
Table games (cards, dice); electronic gaming (Pachinko, slot machines, video poker, video bingo); others (bingo, keno).
Non-casino games
Bingo as played in the US and UK, dead pool, lotteries, pull-tab games, scratch cards, Mahjong, and card games.
Pokémon cards
Cards of fictional characters, bought, sold and pooled by children and adults, with values that vary.
Sports betting
Betting on team sports, such as the football pools played every week in the UK.
Shop contests based on drawing
No one can enter without buying a coupon or merchandise, with the usual price raised so buyers contribute to the prize.
Paid-entry competitions
Paying to call a certain number or send a certain message, or any other financial cost, as in Dream contests and Who Wants to Be a Millionaire.
Bingo is listed twice
The slide places bingo under casino games and under non-casino games. What matters is the wager, not the building it happens in.
SLIDE BLOCK 3

03 The ruling (حكم الميسر)

Gambling is haram, because Allah has forbidden it.

Evidence — Qur’an
O you who believe! Intoxicants (all kinds of alcoholic drinks), gambling, al-Ansaab [sacrifices for idols, etc.] and al-Azlaam [arrows for seeking luck or decision] are an abomination of Shaytaan’s handiwork. So, avoid (strictly all) that (abomination) in order that you may be successful.
SLIDE BLOCK 4

04 What if no money is involved?

Evidence — Qur’an
Satan only wants to cause between you animosity and hatred through intoxicants and gambling and to avert you from the remembrance of Allah and from prayer.

The main reasons for the prohibition are the ones in this verse, not only the money. So a game is not allowed if it causes these sins, even with no monetary wager.

Common mistake
“There’s no money on the table, so it can’t be a problem.” Animosity, hatred and neglect of prayer are enough on their own.
SLIDE BLOCK 5

05 The wisdom behind the ban (حكمة التحريم)

Relying on luck
Earnings come from luck and wishful thinking instead of hard work and the ways Allah prescribed.
Destroying families
Wealth is lost through haram means; rich families become poor and proud souls are humiliated.
Harming health
Stress, illness and nervous breakdowns; it breeds hatred and, in most cases, leads to crime, suicide, insanity and chronic illness.
Bad behavior
It pushes the gambler toward alcohol, drugs, smoking, bad company, cheating and deception.
Mnemonic — FLUB
Families destroyed · Luck replaces work · Unwell in body and mind · Bad behavior follows. Gambling is one big FLUB.
SLIDE BLOCK 6

06 Competitions excluded from gambling

1. Third-party sponsored
The prize comes from a non-participant (a sponsor or donor). Competitors do not contribute to the prize pool, so there is no risk of financial loss.
2. Horse racing, camel racing, archery
Betting or prize allocation in these is permissible based on the hadith below. Jurists unanimously agree on giving and receiving prizes in them.
3. Store vouchers and rewards
Gift vouchers given to customers who buy over a specified amount. The customer pays only for the goods, with no extra charge for entry.
Evidence — hadith
There should be no (money) prizes for competitions except in camel-racing, horse-racing and archery.
Why these three — Imam Ibn al-Qayyim
Allah and His Prophet (PBUH) permitted competition in arrow-shooting and in horse and camel racing in order to encourage Muslims to learn horse-riding and get prepared for Jihad.
Mnemonic — TAG
Third-party sponsor · Archery and racing · Gift vouchers. If a competition has the TAG, it is outside gambling.
Sports betting vs. the exception
Betting on team sports is listed as gambling. The racing and archery exception is limited to what the hadith names, and does not extend to football pools.
SLIDE BLOCK 7

07 Conditions for a permissible competition

1. Legitimate purpose and means
The objectives and methods must be lawful, ethical and serve a good purpose.
2. Lawful prize source
The prize must not be funded by the contestants or a subset of them. The classical exception is horse racing, camel racing and archery or marksmanship.
3. No neglect of obligations
It must not lead to omitting religious or worldly duties, or involve any haram act.
4. Zero-loss participation
Each contestant either wins a prize or leaves without a loss. No one loses original funds or pays a penalty just for taking part.
Mnemonic — check for LOSS
Legitimate purpose · Obligations kept · Source of the prize is lawful · Safe from losing your money.
