01 What a credit card is (بطاقة الائتمان)

A payment card issued to users (cardholders), enabling them to pay for goods and services based on the cardholder’s promise to pay the card issuer the amounts paid plus the other agreed charges.

Credit cards are mainly used for short-term financing, and borrowing limits are pre-set according to the individual’s credit rating.

Benefits

  • Designed for purchases in retail stores or online.
  • Can be used for balance transfers or cash withdrawals.
  • Accepted worldwide, so they work at home and abroad.

02 The three kinds of card (أنواع البطاقات)

Prepaid (covered) Instant credit (charge card) Revolving (renewed) credit
Money comes from Funds loaded before use The issuer pays first; a non-covered card The issuer pays first; a non-covered card
Repayment Deducted straight from the balance In full by the due date (e.g. within 40 days) In installments, subject to interest
Limit renews Only when more funds are loaded Only once the balance is fully settled As long as ongoing payments are maintained
Ruling Permissible Permissible if free of riba conditions and late penalties Not permitted
Do not lump the two non-covered cards together
Instant and revolving credit are both non-covered, but only revolving credit is forbidden by its nature. Interest on the deferred balance is built into how it works.
SLIDE BLOCK 3

03 Prepaid cards (covered) (البطاقة المغطاة)

The cardholder loads funds before use, purchases come straight off the available balance, and the spending limit does not renew until more funds are loaded.

Ruling
Permissible, because they are functionally equivalent to paying in cash and involve no loan (qard).
A question the slide leaves open
The slide asks: what is the ruling on purchasing gold, silver or currencies with this card? It does not answer it. The only stated ruling is for the uncovered card (block 8, point 4), forbidden because of the delayed payment. So the point to settle for a covered card is whether payment is immediate. Confirm the expected answer with your instructor.
SLIDE BLOCK 4

04 Charge cards (non-covered) (البطاقة غير المغطاة)

An instrument issued by a bank to a cardholder under a contract that enables the cardholder to purchase goods or services from a merchant without immediate payment.

The issuer commits to paying the merchant on the cardholder’s behalf, and the cardholder must then settle the balance by a specified deadline.

a. Instant credit
Paid in full by the due date (e.g. within 40 days). No extra charges unless payment is delayed. The limit is not restored until the balance is fully settled.
b. Revolving credit
Repaid in installments subject to interest. The limit renews as long as ongoing payments are maintained.
Read “no extra charges unless delayed” carefully
That delay charge is exactly what block 7 forbids. An instant-credit card that stipulates a late fee does not meet the conditions, even though it charges no interest during the grace period.
SLIDE BLOCK 5

05 Who is involved (أطراف البطاقة)

Cardholder
Makes the purchase and repays the issuer.
Card issuer
The client’s bank.
Global card organization
For example Visa or Mastercard.
Merchant
The seller accepting the card.
Merchant’s bank (acquirer)
Receives the funds for the merchant.
Mnemonic — MAGIC
Merchant · Acquirer · Global organization · Issuer · Cardholder. Five parties make one swipe work.
SLIDE BLOCK 6

06 How a transaction runs (خطوات العملية)

  • Step 1 · PurchaseThe cardholder buys at a store and presents the card.
  • Step 2 · ReceiptThe merchant records the card details and amount, and gives the cardholder a receipt.
  • Step 3 · SubmissionThe merchant compiles the month’s receipts and submits them to their bank.
  • Step 4 · ClearanceThe merchant’s bank requests settlement through the global organization with the issuing bank.
  • Step 5 · Issuer paysThe issuing bank transfers the funds, typically in US dollars, to the global organization at the applicable exchange rate.
  • Step 6 · DisbursementThe global organization pays the merchant’s bank.
  • Step 7 · CollectionThe issuing bank collects what is owed from the cardholder.
SLIDE BLOCK 7

07 What makes a card impermissible (الضوابط الشرعية)

Credit cards may be used provided they are free of elements prohibited by Shariah:

1. Penalty for delay
Stipulating a penalty or extra financial charge for late payment (late fees).
2. Above-cost withdrawal fee
Charging more than the actual administrative cost for cash withdrawals.
3. Revolving credit
Not permitted because it involves interest (riba) on deferred payments.
Mnemonic — PAR
Penalty for delay · Above-cost withdrawal fee · Revolving credit. A permissible card has none of the three.
SLIDE BLOCK 8

08 The Fiqh Council resolution (قرار المجمع الفقهي)

The Fiqh Council’s statement of 2000, no. 108 (1/12), on uncovered cards and bank fees, building on its statements no. 102/4/10 and no. 63/1/7:

  1. Interest stipulated: not permissible. It is not permissible to issue or use a non-covered card if riba or interest is stipulated, even if the holder is determined to pay within the free grace period.
  2. No interest stipulated: permissible. From this the Council deduces: (a) the issuer may charge set fees for issuing or renewing the card, based on the real cost of the services; (b) the bank may take commission from the business on purchases, so long as the business sells at the same price as for cash.
  3. Cash withdrawals are a loan from the issuer. This is acceptable if it leads to no interest and the set fees are not tied to the loan’s amount or period. Anything above the actual cost of services is haram because it is riba (resolutions no. 13 (10/2) and 13 (1/3)).
  4. Gold, silver and currencies: not permissible to buy with the uncovered card, because of the delayed payment.
The sharpest trap in the lecture
“I always pay on time, so I never pay interest.” The ruling looks at the contract, not your intention: if the card stipulates interest, issuing or using it is not permissible.

SLIDE BLOCK 10

10 Working to market credit cards

Marketing a card of a permissible type, free of haram matters, is fine. Marketing a haram type is not permissible, because that helps in sin and transgression.

Evidence — Sahih Muslim 1598, from Jaabir
The Messenger of Allah ﷺ cursed the one who consumes riba and the one who pays it, the one who writes it down and the two who witness it, and he said: they are all the same.
The link to Lecture 7
The same hadith forbids bonds: everyone who enables riba shares the sin, including the person selling the product.
