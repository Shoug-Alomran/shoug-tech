  1. Intermediate Value Theorem: f continuous on [a, b] and K between f(a) and f(b) ⇒ some c in (a, b) has f(c) = K. It is why bisection works.
  2. Taylor: f(x) = Pₙ(x) + Rₙ(x), Rₙ(x) = f⁽ⁿ⁺¹⁾(ξ)(x − x₀)ⁿ⁺¹/(n + 1)!
