Question 2: Parameter cases (3 × 3)

For which $a$ is the solution unique?$$\begin{cases}x_1 + 2x_2 + x_3 = 3 \\ ax_2 + 5x_3 = 10 \\ 2x_1 + 7x_2 + ax_3 = b\end{cases}$$
1 pt

Fractions, decimals, sqrt(…) and lists separated by commas all work.

Worked solution
  1. $R_3 \leftarrow R_3 - 2R_1$: $(0, 3, a - 2 \mid b - 6)$. Then $R_3 \leftarrow aR_3 - 3R_2$:
  2. $$\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}1 & 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & a & 5 & 10 \\ 0 & 0 & a^2-2a-15 & ab-6a-30\end{array}\right]$$
  3. $a^2 - 2a - 15 = (a - 5)(a + 3)$. (At $a = 0$ the scaling step is not allowed, but then the matrix still has three pivots.)

Question 19: Left null space and Aᵀ

1 PT
1
Show that the left null space of $A$ is the null space of $A^T$.
1 pt
Worked solution
  1. The left null space is $\{\mathbf x : \mathbf x^TA = \mathbf 0^T\}$.
  2. Transpose both sides: $(\mathbf x^TA)^T = A^T\mathbf x = \mathbf 0$.
  3. So $\mathbf x$ is in the left null space exactly when $\mathbf x \in \operatorname{Nul}A^T$.

Answer: $\operatorname{LeftNul}A = \operatorname{Nul}A^T$

Question 21: Eigenvalues (2 × 2)

Find the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of $A = \begin{bmatrix}1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4\end{bmatrix}$.
1 pt

Worked solution
  1. $\det(A - \lambda I) = \lambda^2 - 5\lambda - 2 = 0 \Rightarrow \lambda = \tfrac{5 \pm \sqrt{33}}{2}$.
  2. For $\lambda_2 = \tfrac{5 + \sqrt{33}}{2}$: $3v_1 + (4 - \lambda_2)v_2 = 0 \Rightarrow v_1 = \tfrac{\sqrt{33} - 3}{6}v_2$.
  3. For $\lambda_1$: $\mathbf v_1 = \left(\tfrac{-\sqrt{33} - 3}{6},\ 1\right)$.

Answer: $\lambda = \tfrac{5 \pm \sqrt{33}}{2}$

Question 23: Gram-Schmidt

1 PT
1
Apply Gram-Schmidt to the columns of $A = \begin{bmatrix}1 & -2 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 \\ 3 & -2 & 3\end{bmatrix}$.
1 pt

Fractions, decimals, sqrt(…) and lists separated by commas all work.

Worked solution
  1. $\mathbf u_1 = (1, 2, 3)$, $\|\mathbf u_1\|^2 = 14$.
  2. $\mathbf u_2 = \mathbf c_2 - \tfrac{-8}{14}\mathbf u_1 = \left(-\tfrac{10}{7}, \tfrac87, -\tfrac27\right)$, $\|\mathbf u_2\|^2 = \tfrac{24}{7}$.
  3. $\mathbf u_3 = \mathbf c_3 - \tfrac{12}{14}\mathbf u_1 - \tfrac{-8/7}{24/7}\mathbf u_2 = \left(-\tfrac13, -\tfrac13, \tfrac13\right)$.
  4. Check: $\mathbf u_1\cdot\mathbf u_2 = \mathbf u_1\cdot\mathbf u_3 = \mathbf u_2\cdot\mathbf u_3 = 0$.

Answer: Orthogonal basis $\mathbf u_1, \mathbf u_2, \mathbf u_3$
