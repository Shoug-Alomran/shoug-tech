    1. Least-squares solution x̂ minimizes ‖b − Ax‖. Solve the normal equations AᵀAx̂ = Aᵀb. Unique ⇔ A’s columns are independent ⇔ AᵀA invertible: x̂ = (AᵀA)⁻¹Aᵀb.
    TipLeast-squares error = ‖b − Ax̂‖.
