1. True/ False (1 mark)

a)
Ch 1

Well-defined layers interfaces allow changing the implementation of one layer with a completely different implementation without affecting the other layers.

Layers talk only through their interfaces, so one layer's implementation can be replaced while its interface stays the same.
b)
Ch 1

Physical and data link are implemented in hardware in the network interface card (NIC)

The NIC holds the physical and data link layers. The upper layers run in software.
c)
Ch 1

Semantics of protocol means structure or format of the data message, and their order.

That describes syntax. Semantics is the meaning of each section of bits.
d)
Ch 1

National ISPs (Tier-2 ISPs) connect to each other at private peering points.

National ISPs interconnect at IXPs (public peering). Private peering points are how international ISPs connect.

3.
Ch 1

For each of the following networks, write about is the network robust if one computer connection fails?

i. Five devices arranged in a star topology

ii. Five devices arranged in a ring topology

In a star, only the device on the failed link drops out. In a simple ring, a single break stops the signal going around, so the entire network fails.

5.
Ch 1

A new company XYZ has opened in KSA. The company is planning to have two offices; one in Al Waha, Riyadh and other one in Dariya, Riyadh. Company has hired 5 employees for Riyadh office and 4 employees for Jeddah office. The company wants to establish a network between the two offices.

i. Draw a network diagram for the company XYZ two offices using star topology. Sketch it on paper, then compare.

Model answer · 0.5 marks

Each office is a star: its computers connect to one central hub or switch (5 at one office, 4 at the other). The two central devices are then linked to join the offices.

ii. Name the type of network you designed.

Al Waha and Dariya are both in Riyadh, so a network spanning one city is a MAN, not a WAN. The paper mentions Jeddah by mistake: the graded copy corrected WAN to MAN.
