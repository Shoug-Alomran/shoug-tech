If a CRC generator polynomial has a degree of r, how many zero bits are appended to the dataword during CRC encoding?

What is Forward Error Correction (FEC)?

FEC adds enough redundancy for the receiver to repair limited errors by itself, without retransmission.

p-persistent CSMAMark /1.00

Ch 3

In p-persistent CSMA, what step does a station take when it finds the medium idle?

Transmitting with probability 1 is 1-persistent. p-persistent transmits with probability p, otherwise waits one slot and senses again.

CSMA/CDMark /1.00

Ch 3

CSMA/CD is widely used in which network topology when a hub is deployed?

Stations cabled to a hub form a physical star, and the hub makes them share one collision domain, so CSMA/CD applies.
