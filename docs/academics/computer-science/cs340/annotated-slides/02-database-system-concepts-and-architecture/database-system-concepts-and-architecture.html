Concepts, Architecture and Conceptual Design (ER Model)

Levels of abstraction, schemas and instances, logical and physical data independence, then the ER model: entities and attribute types, keys, relationships, degree and cardinality ratios, total vs. partial participation, recursive relationships, weak entities, (min, max) notation and ternary relationships.
