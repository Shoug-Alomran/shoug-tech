Facts: The Divides Relation

Notes

  1. 1

    If a | b and a | c then a | (b + c); if a | b then a | bc; if a | b and b | c then a | c.

Slide 7 / 84

Composite & Prime Numbers

Notes

  1. 1

    Prime: integer > 1 whose only positive divisors are 1 and itself. 1 is NOT prime.

Slide 8 / 84

Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic

Notes

  1. 1

    Every integer > 1 is a unique product of primes (Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic).

Slide 9 / 84

Theorem

Notes

  1. 1

    If n is composite, it has a prime divisor ≤ √n. So test primes up to √n only.

Exam angle

To show 101 is prime it is enough to test divisibility by primes up to:

  1. 50
  2. 10
  3. 7
  4. 101

Greatest Common Division (GCD)

Notes

  1. 1

    gcd(a, b): largest integer dividing both. Relatively prime when gcd = 1.

Slide 15 / 84

2 Ways to find GCD

Notes

  1. 1

    gcd via prime factorizations: take the MIN power of each prime. lcm: take the MAX power.

  2. 2

    gcd(a, b)·lcm(a, b) = a·b.

Exam angle

PredictionQ1

gcd(120, 500) =

  1. 20
  2. 40
  3. 10
  4. 60
Show answer

A. 20 — 2³·3·5 and 2²·5³ → 2²·5.

PredictionQ2

If gcd(a, b) = 6 and ab = 360, lcm(a, b) =

  1. 2160
  2. 54
  3. 60
  4. 36
Show answer

C. 60 — 360 / 6.

The Division “Algorithm”

Notes

  1. 1

    Division algorithm: a = dq + r with 0 ≤ r < d; q = a div d, r = a mod d.

  2. 2

    Trap The remainder is never negative: −11 div 3 = −4, −11 mod 3 = 1 (−11 = 3·(−4) + 1).

Exam angle

PredictionQ1

  1. 2
  2. 1
  3. −1
  4. −2
Show answer

B. 1

Modular Congruence

Notes

  1. 1

    a ≡ b (mod m) when m | (a − b), i.e. a mod m = b mod m.

Affine Ciphers

Notes

RSA Decryption

Exam angle

PredictionQ1

RSA with p = 3, q = 11, e = 3. Find n, φ, and d.

Show answer

n = 33, φ = (p−1)(q−1) = 20. d is the inverse of 3 mod 20: 3·7 = 21 ≡ 1, so d = 7.

PredictionQ2

  2. d
  3. n
  4. e
Show answer

B. d — (e, n) is public.

Diffie-Hellman

Notes

  1. 1

    Diffie–Hellman: agree on prime p and base g; Alice sends gᵃ mod p, Bob sends gᵇ mod p; both compute the shared key gᵃᵇ mod p.

Exam angle

PredictionQ1

p = 23, g = 5, a = 6, b = 15. Alice sends:

  1. 5⁶ mod 23 = 8
  2. 23⁵ mod 6
  3. 6⁵ mod 23
  4. 5¹⁵ mod 23 = 19
Show answer

A. 5⁶ mod 23 = 8
