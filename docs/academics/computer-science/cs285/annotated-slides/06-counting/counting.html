                                          Basic Counting Principles: Subtraction Rule

                                          Subtraction Rule: If a task can be done either in one of n ways

                                          or in one of n ways, then the total number of ways to do the

                                          +

                                          common to the two different ways.

                                                                          Powers of Binomial Expressions

                                                                          • Definition: A binomial expression is the sum of two terms, such as x + y. (More generally, these terms can be products of constants and variables.)
                                                                          • We can use counting principles to find the coefficients in the n expansion of (x + y) where n is a positive integer.
                                                                          • To illustrate this idea, we first look at the process of expanding (x 3 . + y)
                                                                          • (x + y) (x + y) (x + y) expands into a sum of terms that are the product of a term from each of the three sums.
