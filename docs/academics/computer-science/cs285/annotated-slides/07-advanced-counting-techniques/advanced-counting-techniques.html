        Recurrence Relations

        In Chapter 5 we discussed how sequences can be defined recursively.

        Recall that a recursive definition of a sequence specifies:

        1. One or more initial terms and
        2. A rule for determining subsequent terms from those that proceed them. A rule of the latter sort (whether or not it is part of a recursive definition) is called a recurrence relation. such relations can be used in studying and solving counting problems. 4
