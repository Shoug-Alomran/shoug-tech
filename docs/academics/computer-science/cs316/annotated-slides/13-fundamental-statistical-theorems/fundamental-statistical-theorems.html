Exam angle

PredictionQ1

For the rare disease example, P(disease | positive) is about:

  2. 0.99 x 0.001 / 0.011.

PredictionQ2

Why is P(disease | positive) so low?

  1. There are 9.99 false positives against 0.99 true positives.

PredictionQ3

Disease prevalence is 1%, sensitivity 90%, false positive rate 9%. Find P(disease | positive).

Compare with the model answer

P(+) = 0.9(0.01) + 0.09(0.99) = 0.009 + 0.0891 = 0.0981. So P(D|+) = 0.009 / 0.0981, about 0.092, or about 9%.

Central Limit Theorem (CLT)

Exam angle

PredictionQ1

The Central Limit Theorem concerns the distribution of:

  4. Means become approximately normal.

PredictionQ2

Which is a condition for the CLT on the slide?

  2. The population can be any shape.

PredictionQ3

Explain the difference between the Law of Large Numbers and the Central Limit Theorem.

Compare with the model answer

LLN says the sample mean converges to the true mean as the sample grows. CLT says the distribution of sample means is approximately normal for large samples, whatever the population shape.
