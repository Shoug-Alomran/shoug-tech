Slide 5 / 10

Why Values Go Missing: MCAR, MAR, MNAR

Notes

  1. 1

    The table asks why each value is missing. MCAR is pure accident, MAR is explained by other columns, and MNAR is explained by the missing value itself.

  2. 2

    Trap Mean-filling MNAR data can hide the students who most need attention, as the slide warns.

Exam angle

PredictionQ1

Students who registered late have no Major 1 score because they had not yet taken it. This is:

  3. The missingness depends on other observed data, the registration date.

PredictionQ2

A student who failed the final deliberately skips submitting it. This is:

  3. The chance of missing depends on the missing value itself.

PredictionQ3

A project column is skewed by a few zero scores. Which fill is most defensible?

  4. The median is less affected by outliers.

PredictionQ4

Define MCAR, MAR and MNAR.

Compare with the model answer

MCAR: unrelated to any value. MAR: depends on other observed data. MNAR: depends on the missing value itself.

PredictionQ5

Why should you not fill every column with its mean?

Compare with the model answer

The mean is sensitive to outliers and skew, and filling MNAR data can bias results and hide important cases. The right approach depends on what the column means.
