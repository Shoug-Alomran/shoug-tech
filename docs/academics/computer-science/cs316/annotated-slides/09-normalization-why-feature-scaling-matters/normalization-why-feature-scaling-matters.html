PredictionQ3

Describe the correct order of scaling and modeling, and the risk of doing it wrongly.

Compare with the model answer

Split first. Fit the scaler on training data, transform the training and test sets with those statistics, then fit the model. Scaling everything first leaks test information and makes the score too optimistic.
