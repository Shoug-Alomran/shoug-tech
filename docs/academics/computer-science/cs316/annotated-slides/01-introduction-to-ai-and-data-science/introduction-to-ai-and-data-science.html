Data Analytics Types

Exam angle

PredictionQ1

A retailer forecasts next quarter's demand using past sales. This is:

  1. It estimates what will happen. Recommending an order size would be prescriptive.

PredictionQ2

Explain the difference between descriptive, predictive and prescriptive analytics with one example each.

Compare with the model answer

Descriptive says what happened (last month's sales report). Predictive says what will happen (a demand forecast). Prescriptive says what to do next (which driver to dispatch).
