PredictionQ2

Explain the difference between design variables and measurement variables with an example of each.

Compare with the model answer

Design variables are fixed before collection and describe conditions or groups, such as gender, and act as predictors. Measurement variables record what was observed, such as height, and are what we explain or predict.

Quantiles and Percentiles

Exam angle

PredictionQ1

The 0.75 sample quantile is also called:

  2. 25, 50 and 75 percentiles are the first, second and third quartiles.

PredictionQ2

Define the p-sample quantile.

Compare with the model answer

A value x such that at least a fraction p of the data is less than or equal to x and at least a fraction 1-p is greater than or equal to x.
