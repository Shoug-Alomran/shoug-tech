Chapter 1: Introduction to AI and Data Science

Four approaches

  • Acting humanly: Turing test
  • Thinking humanly: model the mind
  • Thinking rationally: logic
  • Acting rationally: best outcome

Big Data 4 Vs

  • Volume: size
  • Velocity: speed
  • Variety: formats
  • Veracity: quality

Process order

  • Collect
  • Clean
  • Explore
  • Engineer features
  • Build
  • Evaluate
  • Deploy and monitor

Do not fall for

  • Passing the Turing test does not prove a machine can think
  • "All AI is machine learning" is false
  • Veracity is about quality, not truth in general
  • Two slides, two lists
  • Correlation is not causation
  • Predictive stops at the forecast

Chapter 2.3: Handling Missing Data

Detect

  • df.isnull()
  • df.isnull().sum()

Fix

  • df.fillna(0)
  • df["a"].fillna(df["a"].mean())
  • df.dropna(axis=0)
  • df.dropna(subset=["final"])
  • df.dropna(thresh=2)

Do not fall for

  • The name "Missing At Random" is misleading
  • Filling with 0 is not neutral
  • thresh counts values that are present

Chapter 3: Statistical Learning

Formulas

  • Squared loss: (y - yhat)²
  • Zero-one loss: I{y != yhat}
  • Risk: E Loss(Y, g(X))
  • Training loss: (1/n) sum Loss(Yi, g(Xi))
  • f(x) = E(Y | X = x)

Learner

  • Choose a class G
  • Minimize training loss in G
  • Judge on an independent test set

Do not fall for

  • "Associated with", not "causes"
  • Optimal does not mean zero error
  • Learner versus optimal function
  • Why you may need three sets

Chapter 4: Foundations of Predictive Modeling

Updates

  • GD: x - alpha grad f(x)
  • Newton: x - H^-1 grad f(x)

Metrics

  • MSE = mean (y - yhat)²
  • RMSE = sqrt(MSE)
  • MAE = mean |y - yhat|

Diagnosis

  • Low train, high test: variance
  • High train, high test: bias
  • Low train, low test: good

Decomposition

  • Error = Bias² + Variance + Irreducible

Do not fall for

  • Learning rate is not "the bigger the better"
  • A lower training error doesn't mean a better model
  • More data does not fix bias

Classification Part 1: Bayes Error and Metrics

Metrics

  • Precision: tp/(tp+fp)
  • Recall: tp/(tp+fn)
  • Specificity: tn/(fp+tn)
  • Accuracy: (tp+tn)/n
  • F1: 2PR/(P+R)

Multilabel

  • Accuracy: |Y and Yhat| / |Y or Yhat|
  • Precision: |Y and Yhat| / |Yhat|
  • Recall: |Y and Yhat| / |Y|
  • Hamming: wrong decisions / (n c)

Do not fall for

  • You can't actually compute g*
  • Which mistake costs more?

Classification Part 2: Binary Classification

Formulas

  • z = b0 + sum bi xi
  • sigma(z) = 1/(1+e^-z)
  • logit(p) = log(p/(1-p))

ROC

  • TPR = TP/(TP+FN)
  • FPR = FP/(FP+TN)
  • Diagonal = random
  • AUC 0.5 random, 1.0 perfect

Do not fall for

  • Regression in the name, classification in the job
  • Dropping num is not optional
  • AUC does not depend on the threshold

Normalization: Why Feature Scaling Matters

Formulas

  • z = (x - mean)/std
  • x' = (x - min)/(max - min)

Scalers

  • Standard: moderate outlier sensitivity
  • MinMax: high
  • Robust: low

Do not fall for

  • fit_transform on the test set

Chapter 7.1: NumPy and Similarity Measures

Formulas

  • x . y = sum xi yi
  • ||x||1 = sum |xi|
  • ||x||2 = sqrt(sum xi²)
  • cos = x . y / (||x|| ||y||)

NumPy

  • np.array
  • np.dot(x, y) or x @ y
  • np.linalg.norm(v)
  • v / np.linalg.norm(v)

Do not fall for

  • The zero vector
  • Cosine similarity is not a distance

Chapter 8: Cross-Validation and Grid Search

K-fold

  • k equal folds
  • Train on k-1, validate on 1
  • Repeat k times
  • Mean and standard deviation

Grid search

  • Parameters: learned
  • Hyperparameters: chosen
  • Fits = combinations x k

Do not fall for

  • The test set stays untouched
  • It is combinations times folds

Chapter 9: Fundamental Statistical Theorems

Formulas

  • Bayes: P(A|B) = P(B|A)P(A)/P(B)
  • MLE: argmax P(data | theta)
  • MAP: argmax P(theta | data)

Theorems

  • LLN: mean converges to mu
  • CLT: means approximately normal
  • Entropy: expected surprise

Do not fall for

  • P(+ | disease) is not P(disease | +)
  • The gambler's fallacy
  • The data doesn't become normal

Tests

  • t = estimate / SE

Errors

  • Type II: false negative, beta

Do not fall for

  • What "95% confident" really means
  • What a p-value is not
