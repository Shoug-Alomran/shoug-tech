Arrays, Linked Lists and Array Lists

Arrays (the scoreboard example, inserting and removing by shifting), singly linked lists (insert/remove at head and tail), doubly linked lists with header and trailer sentinels, array lists, growable arrays (incremental vs. doubling, amortized analysis), positional lists and iterators. The Midterm 1 and Quiz 1 question banks are mostly linked-list code. (This handout carries an earlier student’s handwritten notes.)
