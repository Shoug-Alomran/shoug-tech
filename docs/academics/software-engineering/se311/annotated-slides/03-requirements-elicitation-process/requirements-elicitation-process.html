Slide 14 / 19

When are we done?

Notes

  1. 1

    Done when: users can’t think of more use cases; new ideas are out of scope or low priority; proposed requirements repeat earlier ones; ideas are future, not current; the remaining issues are understood.

Exam angle

Past exam · Quiz 1 (Ch. 1–3)

Q11: when elicitation is done

PredictionQ1

When do you know you are done with elicitation? Give two signs.

Compare with the model answer

Users can’t think of any more use cases or user stories; new suggestions are out of scope or already captured (repeating earlier requirements); remaining ideas are “nice to have” for future releases; the team can explain the requirements well enough to build and test.
