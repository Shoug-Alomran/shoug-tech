3.2 Different, same, matched participant design

Notes

  1. 1

    Designs: different participants (between-subjects: no order effects, needs more people), same participants (within-subjects: fewer people, order effects → counterbalance), matched participants.

  2. 2

    Trap Within-subjects (same participants) designs suffer from ORDER effects; fix with counterbalancing.
