Overview

Interaction and Interfaces

Understanding the problem space (assumptions and claims), conceptual models and interface metaphors, the interaction types (instructing, conversing, manipulating, exploring, responding) and direct manipulation, then a tour of 20 interface types from command-based and WIMP to VR, mobile, speech, touch, tangible, wearable and brain–computer interfaces.
