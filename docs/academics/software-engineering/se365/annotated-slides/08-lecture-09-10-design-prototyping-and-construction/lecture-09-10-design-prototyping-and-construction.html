Notes

2. Conceptual design: from requirements to design

Notes

  1. 1

    Conceptual design: what the product will do and how it behaves (concepts, metaphors, interaction types). Concrete design: colours, layout, icons, details.

  2. 2

    Trap Conceptual design = WHAT and how it behaves; concrete design = how it LOOKS.
