Establishing Requirements

Data gathering (interviews, questionnaires, observation, ethnography, contextual inquiry), recording and guidelines, kinds of requirements, personas and scenarios, use cases and hierarchical task analysis, then data analysis: quantitative and qualitative, grounded theory and activity theory.
