Unit Testing and JUnit

What unit testing is and why, JUnit 5 test classes and methods, verdicts, assertion methods (assertEquals, assertTrue, assertNull, assertSame, assertArrayEquals, assertThrows), floating-point deltas, expected exceptions, and best practices with @BeforeEach, @AfterEach, @BeforeAll, @AfterAll and repeated tests.
