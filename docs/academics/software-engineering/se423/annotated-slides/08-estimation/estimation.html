Exam angle

Past exam · Final 242

Q2.2 · two effort estimation techniques for the solar project

Past exam · Final 251

Q2.1 · estimation techniques and how to validate estimates

PredictionQ1

Choose two effort-estimation techniques for a 12-month hardware/software project and explain how each supports planning and risk control (Final 242).

Compare with the model answer

Bottom-up estimation: decompose the work into a WBS and estimate each task with the people who will do it; this is the most accurate, exposes hidden work and gives a baseline to track against. Three-point (PERT) estimating: for uncertain tasks like prototyping, get optimistic, most likely and pessimistic values and compute (O+4M+P)/6; the spread shows risk and sizes contingency reserves. Alternatives: analogy to past solar projects early on, Wideband Delphi for expert consensus, COCOMO for the software part. Validate estimates by comparing two methods and against historical data, and refine iteratively.

Basic COCOMO – The Modes

Notes

  1. 1

    Organic 2–50 KLOC · Semi-detached 50–300 · Embedded >300 or severe constraints.

  2. 2

    Note c = 2.5 for all three modes.

  3. 3

    Memory trick a goes 2.4 → 3.0 → 3.6 (+0.6 each). d goes 0.38 → 0.35 → 0.32 (−0.03 each). c is always 2.5.

Exam angle

Q2.4 · COCOMO organic, 32 KLOC: effort, time, staff

Past exam · Final 251

Q2.5 · COCOMO organic, 20 KLOC: effort, time, productivity, staff

PredictionQ1

COCOMO organic, 32 KLOC (a=2.4, b=1.05, c=2.5, d=0.38). Compute effort, development time and average staff (Final 242).

Compare with the model answer

E = 2.4 × 32^1.05 ≈ 2.4 × 38.06 ≈ 91.3 person-months. TDEV = 2.5 × 91.3^0.38 ≈ 2.5 × 5.56 ≈ 13.9 months. Average staff = E / TDEV ≈ 91.3 / 13.9 ≈ 6.6 people (about 7).

PredictionQ2

COCOMO organic, 20 KLOC. Compute effort, development time, productivity and staff (Final 251).

Compare with the model answer

E = 2.4 × 20^1.05 ≈ 2.4 × 23.2 ≈ 55.8 person-months. TDEV = 2.5 × 55.8^0.38 ≈ 2.5 × 4.61 ≈ 11.5 months. Productivity = 20,000 LOC / 55.8 PM ≈ 359 LOC per person-month. Staff = 55.8 / 11.5 ≈ 4.8 people (about 5).
