What to study, and what to skip

Skip these for the midterm

Each one either repeats a page above or teaches material that doesn't match the slides.

Skip

Chapter 1 breakdown, Part 1.3: sections 1.3·01–1.3·04

These sections don't follow the slides. The context factors, "design tests" and "common traps" are not in the deck, which teaches the 7 competing demands, the 3 benefits, the 5 things you can tailor and the slide-21 table instead. The "lists the slides test you on" table at the end of Part 1.3 does match the deck, but the cheat sheet has the same lists.

Use instead: Cheat sheet, Chapter 1.3 →
Skip

Team breakdown - Chapter 3

Teaches five conflict "modes" and four decision modes that are not in the slides. The deck lists six techniques: confronting, compromising, withdrawal, smoothing, collaborating, forcing. It also misses the five types of influence, team structure by objective, the team models and staff selection.

Use instead: Cheat sheet, Chapter 3 →
