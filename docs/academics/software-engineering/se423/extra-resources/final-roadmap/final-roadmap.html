02

Look-alikes

These are the pairs and trios that get mixed up. The question under each title is the one that tells them apart.

Project vs product vs program vs portfolio

Ask yourselfTemporary and unique, the thing's whole life, related projects, or everything grouped for strategy?

ConceptTell-tale signExample
ProjectTemporary effort for a unique resultBuild the exam system
Product managementWhole life cycle of the productIntroduction → decline
ProgramRelated projects managed together for benefitsDigital learning improvement
PortfolioProjects, programs, operations for strategic goalsSmart university initiative
StudyBreakdownCh 1SlidesCh 1AnnotatedCh 1Cheat sheetmidterm (instructor questions)
Quick test"Explain product management." What does the trap word change?

Product management spans the product's life cycle and can start projects at any point; project management ends with the project.

Contingency reserve vs management reserve

Ask yourselfIdentified risks or unknown unknowns?

ConceptTell-tale signExample
ContingencyKnown risks, controlled by the PMPlanned rework buffer
ManagementUnknown events, controlled by managementUnplanned in-scope work
