== l1 | 190 | L1–2 | L 1–2 | Foundations: transactions, ijtihad and rights
[grid]
[card The original ruling (most tested in L1)]
- **Worship:** forbidden unless a text permits it.
- **Transactions:** permitted unless a clear text forbids them.
- Constant in transactions: consent, no injustice, preserving wealth. Flexible: the methods (money moved from gold and silver to paper).
[/card]
[card Contemporary financial transactions]
Cases that emerged in the modern age, changed by progress or circumstances, carry new names, or combine old forms.
1. **Unknown before:** companies, banknotes.
2. **Rules updated by progress:** taking possession by handing over a key → property registry.
3. **New names for old rulings:** bank interest = riba.
4. **Combined old forms:** murabaha, leasing.
Jurists' terms: nawazil (emerged cases), waqi'at (incidences), occurrences, fatwa.
[/card]
[card Four categories of transactions]
- **Bargains:** sale, leasing.
- **Donations:** grants, will, waqf.
- **Dropping (isqat):** ending a debt.
- **Documentation (tawthiq):** mortgage, bail, draft.
[/card]
[card Key texts]
- An-Nisa 4:29: do not consume wealth unjustly; trade by mutual consent.
- Al-Baqarah 2:275: Allah permitted trade and forbade riba.
- The prohibition of the gharar sale.
- "Muslims keep to their conditions…"
- Five necessities (maqasid): faith, life, intellect, lineage, **wealth**.
[/card]
[card Rights (L2)]
- **Right (haqq):** an exclusive entitlement by which the Shari'ah establishes an authority or an obligation.
- **Political:** election, nomination.
- **Civil → general:** body, home. **Civil → private → family:** custody, divorce. **Private → financial:** personal (creditor–debtor), material (property), **incorporeal** (innovation, intellectual property).
!tip Classify: "a creditor's claim" = personal financial; "owning a car" = material; "an author's right over a book" = incorporeal; "right to vote" = political.
[/card]
[card Ijtihad]
- Nine qualifications: Quran, Sunnah, ijma', Arabic, talent for ijtihad, honesty, maqasid, deriving from scholars' books, understanding the lived reality.
- Eight steps for a new case: pray → understand the case → Quran and Sunnah → Companions → four imams → recent scholars → own opinion → otherwise refrain.
[/card]
[/grid]

== l3 | 150 | L3 | L 03 | Incorporeal rights: copyright, patents, trade names
[grid]
[card Why intellectual property is legitimate (repeated)]
1. Benefits are compensable (they are property, mal).
2. Custom ('urf) recognizes them.
3. Plagiarism is prohibited.
4. Rights correspond to responsibilities ("the benefit runs with the burden").
!trap For **patents** the list omits custom ('urf).
[/card]
[card Copyright]
- Berne Convention 1886.
- **Literary right:** permanent, not transferable (the name stays the author's).
- **Financial right:** temporary, inheritable, sellable.
- Mere collecting without creativity is not protected.
[/card]
[card Patent (1791)]
- Rights: use it for a limited time; carry the inventor's name.
- Certificate types: **F**ull rights, **L**imited, **A**dditive (improvements), **I**mportation.
!hook F.L.A.I.
[/card]
[card Trade name (1909)]
- Components: **M**ark (trademark), **S**ign (shop sign), **L**ocation.
- Trademark functions: distinctiveness, attraction, surveillance.
- Rights: exclusive use; a transferable asset. Lawful unless built on cheating or gharar.
!hook M.S.L.
[/card]
[/grid]

== l4 | 262 | L4 | L 04 | Insurance: commercial vs collaborative
> The single most tested lecture (insurance definition, forms, ruling and the comparison all repeat).
[grid]
[card Meaning and philosophy]
A contractual system of compensation or donation; at heart, **collective risk-sharing**.
[/card]
[card Commercial insurance: why prohibited]
1. Excessive **gharar** (uncertainty in what you get back).
2. **Gambling** (pay a little, may get much or nothing).
3. **Both ribas:** getting more back = riba al-fadl; getting the same back later = riba al-nasi'ah.
Exceptions: when it is **dependent, not original** (included in a ticket or car rental), **necessity** (legally required), or **free**.
[/card]
[card Commercial insurance contract]
- Elements: insurer, insured, risk, installment (premium), insurance amount.
- Risk must be: uncertain, not intentional, not prohibited, future, regular.
- Types: people (life, casualty), property, liability.
[/card]
[card Collaborative (takaful) insurance]
- Root: Al-Ma'idah 5:2 "cooperate in righteousness and piety".
- Forms in Islam: the people of zakat, kinship ('aqilah, blood money), social solidarity (the Ash'arites pooling food).
- Systems (all lawful): **retirement** (a donation; workers are insurer and insured), **social security**, **reciprocal** (mutual funds).
[/card]
[/grid]
[table]
 | Commercial | Collaborative
Ruling | Prohibited | Encouraged
Contract | Compensation (exchange) | Donation
Aim | Profit for the company | Solidarity
Surplus | Kept by the company | Returned to participants
Relationship | Company vs customer | Participants insure each other
[/table]

== l5 | 205 | L5–7 | L 5–7 | Stock market, shares and bonds
[grid]
[card Stock market (L5)]
- The bourse began in Bruges (1285). Brokers trade for clients; dealers trade for themselves.
- Instruments: stocks, bonds, commodities, foreign exchange.
- Regular market vs stock market: face to face vs brokers; goods present vs elsewhere; inspect first vs not; real sales vs speculation.
- Securities: **title deeds = shares** (ownership), **debt deeds = bonds** (loans).
[/card]
[card Shares (L6)]
- Equal value, indivisible, tradable, have a nominal value.
- Four values: **nominal** (par), **issue** (IPO price), **real** (assets − liabilities), **market** (supply and demand).
- Conditions to deal: halal activity, no riba, known transparent company, not preferred, not enjoyment shares; no riba loans, no short selling; installments allowed.
- Zakat: investor → the company pays; trader → on market value at year end.
[/card]
[card Prohibited share types]
- **Preferred shares:** fixed profit regardless of results (riba) and unequal treatment of partners.
- **Enjoyment shares:** redeemed early by lottery, then profit is taken without capital (unjust, gharar).
[/card]
[card Bonds (L7)]
- Transferable deeds representing long-term loans with interest; equal, nominal value, transferable, indivisible.
- Names: investment certificates (increasing value, current yield, lottery prizes), treasury bills (~3 months, tenders, very liquid), loan bonds.
- **Prohibited: riba.** A lottery prize on top is still interest on a loan.
- Premium-redeemable: nominal 1,000 SAR, paid 900, the 100 comes back at maturity.
- Alternatives: companies issue shares or partnerships; governments use good loans, advancing zakat, donations, exceptional taxes.
[/card]
[/grid]
[table]
 | Shares | Bonds
Represent | Ownership of capital | A debt (loan)
Return | Depends on profit | Fixed interest
Vote | Yes | No
At dissolution | Paid after debts | Paid first
Ruling | Permissible with conditions | Prohibited (riba)
[/table]

== l8 | 330 | L8–9 | L 8–9 | Leasing, installment sale and murabaha
[grid]
[card Leasing (ijarah)]
- Sale of a known **benefit** for a specified period at an agreed rent; the asset itself is not sold.
- **Lessor (owner):** deliver a usable asset, bear defects and structural repairs, reclaim at the end.
- **Lessee (user):** pay the rent, routine maintenance, return the asset.
- Evidence includes the hadith qudsi against one who hires a worker and withholds his wages.
[/card]
[card Lease ending with ownership]
- Not allowed as commonly done: rent above market, unclear whether it is a sale or a lease, unfair loss to the client on default.
- Permissible if: fixed period, fixed installments with no increase for lateness, ownership transferred by **gift or nominal sale** in a separate contract, no down payment.
[/card]
[card Sale and installment sale]
- Seven conditions of a sale: mutual consent, legal capacity, lawful item, ownership, deliverability, knowledge of goods and price, immediate execution.
- **Installment sale:** a higher price for deferral is permissible if the price is fixed at the contract (50,000 cash or 60,000 over a year).
[/card]
[card Murabaha]
1. **Promise** from the client (not binding before the bank buys).
2. The bank **buys and owns** the item.
3. The bank **sells it at a profit** to the client.
Conditions: price fixed; the profit inside the price; no extra for late payment; not a ribawi item (gold, dates); guarantee or mortgage allowed.
!trap If the bank sells before it owns the item, or adds a late-payment increase, it becomes riba.
[/card]
[/grid]
[table]
 | Installment sale | Lease to own | Standard lease
Contracts | One | Two (lease, then transfer) | One
Ownership | Passes now | Passes at the end | Never
[/table]

== l10 | 32 | L10 | L 10 | Credit cards
> New for the final.
[grid]
[card Three card types]
- **Covered (prepaid):** you load money first and spend it. **Permissible**: like cash, no loan.
- **Instant credit (charge):** paid in full by the due date (about 40 days). **Permissible** if no riba and no late penalties are stipulated.
- **Revolving credit:** installments with interest, the limit renews. **Not permitted** (riba).
[/card]
[card Fiqh Council resolution 108]
- Card stipulating interest: not permissible, **even if you plan to pay within the grace period**.
- No interest stipulated: permissible.
- Issue and renewal fees: allowed at real cost.
- Merchant commission: allowed if the item is sold at the cash price.
- Cash withdrawal is a loan: anything above actual cost is riba.
- No buying gold, silver or currencies with an uncovered card (delayed exchange).
[/card]
[card Five parties and the flow]
**MAGIC**: **M**erchant, **A**cquirer (merchant's bank), **G**lobal organization (Visa/Mastercard), **I**ssuer (cardholder's bank), **C**ardholder.
Purchase → receipt → merchant submits to its bank → clearance through the global organization → issuer pays → merchant's bank credited → issuer collects from the cardholder.
[/card]
[card What makes a card impermissible]
!hook **PAR**: **P**enalty or extra charge for late payment · **A**bove-actual-cost cash withdrawal fee · **R**evolving credit.
[/card]
[/grid]

== l11 | 45 | L11 | L 11 | Gambling
> New for the final.
[grid]
[card Ruling and wisdom]
- Haram by the Qur'an.
- Not allowed even with no money if it causes animosity, hatred or neglect of prayer.
!hook Why banned, **FLUB**: **F**amilies destroyed, **L**uck replaces work, **U**nwell in body and mind, **B**ad behaviour.
[/card]
[card Gambling vs betting]
- **Gambling:** playing games of chance for money.
- **Betting:** risking money on a future event decided by other people's effort (tonight's match).
[/card]
[card Forms gambling takes]
Casino games · non-casino games (lotteries, scratch cards) · Pokémon cards · sports betting · shop draws that require a purchase (raised prices fund the prize) · paid-entry competitions (calls or messages).
[/card]
[card Permissible competitions]
- Excluded from gambling, **TAG**: **T**hird-party sponsored prize, **A**rchery and racing (horse, camel), **G**ift vouchers from a shop.
- Conditions, **LOSS**: **L**egitimate purpose, **O**bligations not neglected, **S**ource of the prize is not the contestants, **S**afe from losing your money.
[/card]
[/grid]

== l12 | 280 | L12 | L 12 | Multi-level marketing and pyramid schemes
> New for the final.
[table]
 | Multi-level marketing | Pyramid scheme
What it is | A marketing strategy with real products | A fraud based on recruitment fees
Income | Commission on own sales and recruits' sales | Money from new recruits
Products | Real (Avon, Discovery Toys, Telecom Plus) | Often fake or a front
Law | Legal | Illegal
Islam | **Forbidden** | **Forbidden**
[/table]
[grid]
[card Why forbidden]
The real drive is the commission, not the product. **GUT**: **G**harar, **U**sury (riba), **T**aking people's money unjustly. Early joiners profit from later layers.
[/card]
[card How to make it permissible]
**DUG**: **D**irect-sale commissions only (no multi-tier payouts), no **U**pfront costs, **G**enuine fairly priced products.
[/card]
[/grid]

== test | 340 | Self-test | TEST | Check your understanding
[qs]
? Practice (L10) :: Sara loads 2,000 riyals onto a card and spends only from that balance. Ruling?
= Permissible: it works like cash and involves no loan.

? Practice (L10) :: A card must be paid in full within 40 days and the limit returns once settled. Which card?
= Instant credit (charge card)

? Practice (L10) :: A customer repays monthly with interest and the limit renews. Ruling?
= Not permitted: riba on deferred payment (revolving credit).

? Practice (L10) :: A card stipulates interest after the grace period, but you always pay on time. Ruling?
= Not permissible: agreeing to the riba condition is itself the problem (resolution 108).

? Practice (L11) :: Placing money on who will win tonight's match is…
= Betting: risking money on an event decided by others' effort.

? Practice (L11) :: A shop raises its prices and enters each buyer in a prize draw. Classification?
= Gambling, because the buyers fund the prize.

? Practice (L11) :: A company sponsors a quiz with free entry and a prize from its own budget. Ruling?
= Permissible: third-party sponsored, no loss to contestants (TAG, LOSS).

? Practice (L12) :: A distributor earns on her own sales and a percentage on her recruits' sales. This describes…
= Multi-level marketing (forbidden: commission-driven, gharar).

? Midterm style (L4) :: Which is NOT a reason commercial insurance is prohibited?
- Gharar
- Gambling
- Riba
- It is a donation contract
= d) It is a donation contract
+ Donation is the basis of collaborative insurance, which is encouraged.

? Midterm style (L4) :: Car insurance included in a rental price is…
= Permitted as an exception: dependent, not original.

? Midterm style (L3) :: Which reason for the legitimacy of intellectual property is left out for patents?
= Custom ('urf)

? Midterm style (L2) :: An author's right over his book is which kind of right?
= Incorporeal (financial, private) right

? Midterm style (L7) :: Investment certificates with lottery prizes are…
= Prohibited: still interest on a loan, with a lottery added.

? Midterm style (L6) :: Why are preferred shares prohibited?
= Fixed profit regardless of results (riba) and unequal treatment of partners.

? Midterm style (L8) :: In leasing, what is transferred to the lessee?
= A known benefit of the asset for a specified period (not ownership).

? Midterm style (L9) :: The client promised to buy, but the bank sells the car before buying it. What is wrong?
= The bank must own the item before selling it; selling what you do not own makes the deal invalid (and a disguised loan).

? Midterm style (L1) :: Opening a bank account as a new kind of contract: what is the default ruling for a new transaction?
= Permissible unless a clear text forbids it.
[/qs]
